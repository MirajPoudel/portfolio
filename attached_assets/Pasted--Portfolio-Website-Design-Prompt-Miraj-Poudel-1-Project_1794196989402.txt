## 2. Design Direction

Create a minimal, premium, professional design with a dark visual theme.

### Color palette
- Primary background: Deep black (#09090F)
- Secondary background: Dark charcoal (#11111B)
- Primary accent: Purple (#8B5CF6)
- Secondary accent: Electric blue (#3B82F6)
- Primary text: White (#F8FAFC)
- Secondary text: Muted gray (#A1A1AA)
- Borders: Subtle dark gray

### Visual style
- Minimal and clean layouts.
- Purple and blue gradient accents.
- Subtle glassmorphism for selected cards.
- Smooth scrolling and elegant transitions.
- Soft glow effects on interactive elements.
- Modern typography such as Inter, Sora, or Space Grotesk.
- Rounded corners and subtle borders.
- Generous whitespace.
- Avoid excessive gradients, glowing effects, and unnecessary visual clutter.

The website should feel elegant, technical, and professional.
